The Everlasting Friendships

In English they say, “friends forever,” But in poetry, we say, “You’re my smile in the darkest days, The light that never fades away.”

In every world, in every life, You, my friend, I will never forget. For every memory and every moment, You’re someone I will always respect.

Through highs and lows, hand in hand, Together we’ll forever stand. In friendship’s glow, we find our way, With you, my friend, each day’s a brighter day.
