The seat beside me

Loneliness used to sit beside me at lunch, Quietly taking the seat no one else wanted. The halls knew my footsteps better than they knew my name, And the walk home always felt longer than it should.

Then one day, something has changed, Friendship knocked on my door without even saying hello. It just walked into my life. Bringing laughter to the places silence used to own.

Time started running faster when we were together, And even ordinary days began to feel like memories. Now, even though life keeps pulling us in different directions, I am glad loneliness has finally learned how to let me go.
