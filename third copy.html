MY SUMMARY ON MY MOCK INTERVIEW, BY JAMES GABRIELLE VILLARIZA

Summary The mock interview covered the interviewee's experience at the STEM-B strand. The interviewee explained that STEM-B was chosen due to the expectations from their family about them becoming doctors as well as because of the flexibility of this strand. According to the interviewee, STEM-B allows him/her to be independent and get prepared for the workload he/she will face in college as well as during being a medical student. The most challenging part for the interviewee is to cope with subjects requiring much work, and this problem is solved by means of proper time management, but with some play time first.
