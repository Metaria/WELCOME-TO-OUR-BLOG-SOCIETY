MY REFLECTION EFFECTIVE COMMUNICATION BY JAMES GABRIELLE VILLARIZA

I learned that communication can face various barriers that hinder effective delivery and, therefore, one needs to acquire knowledge on tackling them. I have also discovered that communication encompasses a wide realm of meanings beyond just words, including facial expressions, body language, intonation, and gestures. I have further learned the significance of oral traditions as a carrier of stories, cultural heritage, and knowledge from one generation to another. Additionally, I have realized the creative use of words through spoken word poetry to articulate feelings, opinions, and thoughts creatively. The interviewing skills taught are essential in questioning and seeking information from people while being responsible with the provided information. Finally, I have learned that journal writing is an incredible tool for venting and introspection to documentations reflections, ideas, and lessons learned simultaneously. Conclusively, I have gained a wealth of knowledge on the art of communication while learning diverse and unique aspects of the interviewing process. I have learned that communication entails active listening and respect for others’ viewpoints to convey a message appropriately.
